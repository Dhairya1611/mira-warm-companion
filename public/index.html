Plain-language privacy

What happens to your signals?

  • Expression & postureMediaPipe analyses frames locally. Mira shows broad cues, never a diagnosis.
  • Vocal energyYour browser measures volume locally. It does not decide your emotion.
  • AI conversationIf you connect Gemini, audio and up to one camera frame per second can be sent to Google under its free-tier data terms.
  • Optional memoryWhen you sign in, Mira saves conversation text and only broad, tentative cue labels to your private account so the next visit can continue naturally.
  • Never storedRaw video, audio, camera frames, face landmarks, voiceprints, and biometric templates are never saved. The permanent Gemini key stays in encrypted server configuration.
  • Your controlOpen “What Mira remembers” at any time to review or erase your saved history. Private visits are not retained after you leave.
